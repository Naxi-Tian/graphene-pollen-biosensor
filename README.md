# Graphene Pollen Biosensor

This research project developed graphene-based electrochemical sensors for detecting pollen allergens. From December 2024 through June 2026, Tenne Tian fabricated, chemically modified, and characterized the sensors and compared results with hospital and weather-station field data.

The work produced an 18,500-word research paper published in *Advances in Materials Physics and Chemistry* and a 40,000-word experimental log. The broader ISEF project received a 2026 Regeneron ISEF Grand Award in Biomedical Engineering.

> This repository is a public-facing research package template. It does not include experimental files or reproduce the paper's results yet.

## Research scope

- Graphene-based electrochemical sensor fabrication.
- Chemical modification for pollen-allergen detection.
- Sensor characterization, including work involving electrochemical instrumentation, SEM, and AFM.
- Comparison with hospital and weather-station field data.
- Iterative experimental documentation.
